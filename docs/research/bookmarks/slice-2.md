## Recommendations after deduplication

**No new deterministic checks.** Raw-versus-rendered content, metadata, bot policy, status/redirect health, sitemap quality and internal discovery already have catalog entries. A single crawl cannot establish actual indexing, willingness to pay, backlinks' value, historical traffic, keyword demand or AI answer inclusion. User-agent spoofing does not verify bot reachability through a WAF. See the [existing catalog](../03-check-catalog.md).

LLM rubrics should distinguish visible attribution from verified truth, scope firsthand/comparative claims, and report uncertainty rather than infer search demand or conversion lift from copy. Coding-agent prompts should preserve verified source facts, reject H1-only/name-swap programmatic pages, and keep paid placement, organic discovery, training access and retrieval observations distinct.

The useful operational addition is a change/readback record: affected URL, owner/change date, source metrics, baseline and comparison windows, segmentation and caveats. Seasonality, campaigns, indexing lag and unrelated edits can explain movement. Treat results as unproven when evidence is missing; do not promise a fixed uplift or evaluation window. This is adapted from the source workflow, not evidence that its priority formula or thresholds work universally.

## Deferred product ideas

- Keyword/competitor opportunity planning needs authenticated or licensed demand data and transparent locale/date provenance. Existing HTML checks must not invent that data.
- Verified edge-access testing and paid-crawl policy inspection need provider configuration/logs and publisher intent; never enable charging or change training access as an automatic repair.
- Conversion experimentation needs analytics or experiment allocation and a measured outcome. Do not turn urgency, social-proof presence or form count into guaranteed conversion prescriptions.
- The repository's marketing scripts, remote telemetry, API adapters and weighted scoring were not imported. Small evidence and measurement guardrails were integrated instead; see [integration decisions](./integration.md).

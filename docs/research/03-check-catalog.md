# SEO + AEO + GEO audit check catalog

Research snapshot: 2026-10-20. This is an implementation catalog of 97 deduplicated checks. `kind=deterministic` means the result is computable from the listed crawl artifacts; `kind=llm` requires semantic review and must retain an evidence excerpt. Scope `site` aggregates pages or host policy. `data needed` names the minimum evidence, not an exhaustive fetch plan.

## Scoring and interpretation

- Each check has a weight from 1–5 (5 = greatest expected impact). Weight is an audit prioritization value, not a Google or AI-platform ranking factor. Severity is per finding: critical blocks the intended result broadly; high materially harms discovery or accuracy; medium is a meaningful quality gap; low is advisory/optional.
- Pass/warn/fail cutoffs below are reproducible engine defaults. Unless explicitly identified as a protocol, Google eligibility rule, or Core Web Vitals target, numeric cutoffs are operational heuristics, not official ranking thresholds. Record measured values and evidence alongside status. `N/A` applies when the page/type/business context does not apply; do not penalize absence of optional features.
- Never infer page quality or legitimacy from word count alone. Do not create facts, reviews, credentials, schema claims, or crawler permission. Robots directives are preferences for compliant crawlers, not access control.
- GEO quality signals do not guarantee citation or recommendation. Keep live assistant-query tests as sampled observations, with platform, time, query, response, and citations retained; do not collapse them into a purported universal “AI visibility score.”
- Local source mining: all files under the requested `seo-ai-visibility` skill were read: `SKILL.md`, `reference/ai_visibility.md`, `audit_checklist.md`, `catalog_refresh.md`, `content_rewrite.md`, and `gotchas.md`. `.claude/skills` and `.claude/plugins` were searched for SEO/schema/AEO/GEO-related names; no additional relevant skills were found (matches were unrelated Office schema XSD files). Sources are identified inline as **Local skill** or linked public primary/first-party sources. Public skill/repository examples were cross-checked for coverage, not treated as normative technical authority: [AgriciDaniel/claude-seo](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo), [Bright Data SEO audit skill](https://github.com/brightdata/skills/tree/main/skills/seo-audit), [Lighthouse SEO audit reference](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/seo/).
